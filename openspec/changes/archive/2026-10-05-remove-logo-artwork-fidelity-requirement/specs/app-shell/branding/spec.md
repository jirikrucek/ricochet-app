## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Logo faithfully reproduces the official artwork
**Reason**: A pixel-level fidelity check on the logo artwork is not necessary. The remaining branding requirements cover what matters: the logo is shown, links home, has a stable accessible name, stays sharp and fits the nav bar.
**Migration**: None. The logo asset and its rendering are unchanged; only the requirement and its scenario "Logo matches the official artwork" are dropped, together with the e2e test and screenshot baseline that verified them.
